StackF765: STM32F765VIT6 flight controller, ArduPilot board name StackF765, 2S–3S, 50 A. Upper (flight controller) and Lower (power) boards: 31.5 × 48 mm on 8.0 mm standoffs; Upper top surface 9.6 mm above the Lower’s. Rev B. Soft-mounted, Matek style: a silicone grommet is pressed into each of the Lower board’s four 4 mm holes (20 × 20 mm pattern) and an M2 screw with a washer goes through each grommet into the frame, so the Lower board floats on rubber 2.5 mm above the plate. The Upper board is not fastened to the frame; it sits on the Lower board’s three standoffs. Interboard connectors, fitted by JLCPCB: Samtec SFMC-110-02-L-D-K-TR (Lower J1) / FTSH-110-04-F-DV-TR (Upper J3); standoffs Sinhoo SMTSO2080CTJ, 8 mm. Upper J1 is a 3×12 right-angle header, body overhanging the front edge; it is the only hand-soldered part. Lower J2 is 3×9 labelled solder holes with no header: wires are soldered straight in. Service board (USB / buzzer / boot) is on a 100 mm cable and can be mounted anywhere on the airframe; shown loose. It is 18 × 32 mm; the cable is six-wire JST-SH.
Rubber grommet mounting
Four silicone grommets in the Lower board’s 4 mm holes · 20 × 20 mm · M2 screws into the frame. View “Mounting” shows them side-on.

Roof hidden to show the FC on its four silicone grommets; frame posts are 30 mm.

KiCad board models with standard dark-green display solder mask, black headers and gold pins applied as display materials. No synthetic pad solids, overlaid board textures or altered board geometry. The orange silicone grommets (generic “M3 to M2” type: 4 mm waist, 6.6 mm flanges, 6.6 mm tall), washers and M2 screws are nominal illustrations, as is the cable route to the loose service board, which lies on the ground grid beside the frame and is not fixed to it. No motors, ESC, battery or other wiring. Design verified by rule checks; no hardware built yet.

Design notes
Current geometry and display limits

Boards are the GLB models KiCad exports from the design files, placed by rigid transforms only: Lower PCB 2.5–4.1 mm above the bottom plate on the lower flanges of its grommets, 8.0 mm standoffs, 31.5 × 48 mm outlines. Colours are display materials, not a fabrication finish. Header visibility toggles the Upper J1 header model; the interboard connector stays visible. The silicone grommets, M2 screws and cable are simple drawn shapes, not CAD; frame-side nuts are not drawn. The Matek option is a separate historical photo-derived reference.
