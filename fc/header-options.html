StackF765: lower Dupont header options

Decision pending (2026-11-25): these are options, not the current design. The current boards have bare solder holes at this position.

StackF765: four ways to fit a Dupont pin header on the lower board. Clearance is between the upper Dupont header solder tails and the nearest metal of the lower Dupont header, at nominal dimensions. All dimensions in mm. Click an image for full size.

1. Underside

The same 3x9 right-angle header soldered on the bottom of the lower board, pins forward.

Clearance
5.2 mm nominal
What changes
No board change, same Gerbers. Same holes and pin-to-net map; the rows are upside down seen from the front (signal lowest).
The catch
Hangs 7.5 mm below the lower board at the front; the frame must be open there.

2. 11 mm gap

Header on top of the lower board; boards moved 3 mm further apart.

Clearance
3.0 mm nominal
What changes
Standoffs (11 mm) and the upper board-to-board connector change.
The catch
Stack 3 mm taller; the new connector is not stocked by JLCPCB.

3. Cut tails

Header on top, 8 mm gap; the upper Dupont header solder tails are cut to 0.5 mm.

Clearance
0.9 mm (0.26 worst case) nominal
What changes
None.
The catch
Tight, and depends on trimming every board.

4. Two-row

A 2x9 right-angle header on top, in the two hole rows nearest the edge; 8 mm gap.

Clearance
2.5 mm nominal
What changes
Lower board re-routed.
The catch
No 5 V pins on this connector.

Reference: as first designed (rejected)

3-row header on top of the lower board, 8 mm gap, upper Dupont header solder tails as supplied (1.4 mm). The tail tips are level with the tops of the lower Dupont header pins and 0.63 mm to the side of them.

About the pictures

The two boards, the upper Dupont header with its solder tails, the standoffs and the board-to-board connector are the project's own 3D models. The lower Dupont header, the Dupont plugs and wires are drawn from the header drawing's dimensions (0.64 mm pins at 1.21 / 3.75 / 6.29 mm, 7.5 mm body; 5.0 mm body for the two-row part). In option 1's side view the frame plate is drawn 2.5 mm below the lower board (grommet height); its thickness and where it ends are illustrative. In option 2 the top 3 mm of each standoff and the taller board-to-board connector are drawn stand-ins. In option 3 the model's tails are clipped at 0.5 mm. In the front views the lower Dupont header's plastic body is drawn transparent so the pins and tails behind it can be seen. Side and front views are parallel projections, to scale.
