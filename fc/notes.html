StackF765 render notes

The StackF765 Upper, Lower and Service board models share one loader across the Drone, Board and comparison pages. The two boards are 8.0 mm apart on standoffs. The Lower board is soft-mounted, Matek style: silicone grommets in its four 4 mm holes (20 × 20 mm pattern) with M2 screws into the frame; grommets and screws are drawn as nominal shapes. Service board (USB / buzzer / boot) is on a 100 mm cable and can be mounted anywhere on the airframe; shown loose. Design verified by rule checks; no hardware built yet.

Display-only dark-green solder mask, black headers and gold pins are materials applied to the board models KiCad exports. The exported geometry is unchanged. The 31.5 × 48 mm manufacturing outlines in the design files govern clearance, not this render.

STM32F765VIT6, ArduPilot board name StackF765, 2S–3S, 50 A. Rev B. Interboard connectors, fitted by JLCPCB: Samtec SFMC-110-02-L-D-K-TR (Lower J1) / FTSH-110-04-F-DV-TR (Upper J3); standoffs Sinhoo SMTSO2080CTJ, 8 mm. Upper J1 is a 3×12 right-angle header, body overhanging the front edge; it is the only hand-soldered part. Lower J2 is 3×9 labelled solder holes with no header: wires are soldered straight in. The Matek option remains its separately disclosed photograph-derived reference. External-header visibility toggles the Upper J1 header model; the interboard pair stays visible.

Renders generated from the KiCad design files at github.com/PeterJBurke/stackf765. A rendered assembly is not a fabrication-release claim.
