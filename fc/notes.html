StackF765 render notes

The StackF765 Upper, Lower and Service board models share one loader across the Drone, Board and comparison pages. The two boards are 8.0 mm apart on standoffs. The Lower board is soft-mounted, Matek style: silicone grommets in its four 4 mm holes (20 × 20 mm pattern) with M2 screws into the frame; grommets and screws are drawn as nominal shapes. Service board (USB / buzzer / boot) is on a 100 mm cable and can be mounted anywhere on the airframe; shown loose. Design verified by rule checks; no hardware built yet.

Display-only dark-green solder mask, black headers and gold pins are materials applied to the board models KiCad exports. The exported geometry is unchanged. The 31.5 × 48 mm manufacturing outlines in the design files govern clearance, not this render.

STM32F765VIT6, ArduPilot board name StackF765, 2S–3S, 50 A. Rev C. Board-to-board connector, fitted by JLCPCB: Samtec SFMC-110-02-L-D-K-TR (socket, Lower board) / FTSH-110-04-F-DV-TR (header, Upper board), 4.5 mm forward of the board centre; standoffs Sinhoo SMTSO2080CTJ, 8 mm. Both Dupont headers are on the Upper board, top side, bodies overhanging the edges: the upper front Dupont header (3×12, UARTs, I2C, video) and the upper rear Dupont header (3×9; seen from behind: PI5V PI5V M1 M2 M3 M4 M5 M6 LED; top pin signal, middle 5 V, bottom ground). They are the only hand-soldered parts besides the four battery/ESC wires on the Lower board, which has no pin header and no header holes. The microSD socket is under the Upper board; the card slides in from the left-hand edge. The Matek option remains its separately disclosed photograph-derived reference. External-header visibility toggles the two Dupont header models on the Upper board; the board-to-board connector stays visible.
