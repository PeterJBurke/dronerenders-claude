Lower boards, shared scale

StackF765: 31.5 × 48 mm. Matek F405-WMO: 22 × 32 mm. Both sit in the same millimetre scene, with a 6 mm gap between PCB edges.

The StackF765 board is the model KiCad exports from the design files, with its pad artwork. Matek uses the reference model and manufacturer photographs: it is a 3D reconstruction, with some component dimensions and heights estimated. It is not manufacturer CAD.

Top view is orthographic. The same camera and zoom apply to both boards in every view. In 3D, foreshortening changes the apparent outline, not the model dimensions.

Matek lower is oriented with its large power pads at the same −Y end as the StackF765 board.
