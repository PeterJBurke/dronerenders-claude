StackF765 boards

31.5 × 48 mm boards on 8.0 mm standoffs: Upper top surface 9.6 mm above the Lower’s. Upper is the flight controller (STM32F765VIT6, ArduPilot board name StackF765); Lower is the power board (2S–3S, 50 A). Rev B. Interboard connectors: Samtec SFMC-110-02-L-D (Lower J1) / FTSH-110-04-L-DV (Upper J3); standoffs Sinhoo SMTSO2080CTJ, 8 mm. Upper J1 is a 3×12 and Lower J2 a 3×9 right-angle header, bodies overhanging the front edge. Service board (USB, buzzer, boot): 18 × 32 mm on a 100 mm six-wire JST-SH cable. Design verified by rule checks; no hardware built yet.

Soft-mounted, Matek style: a silicone grommet is pressed into each of the Lower board’s four 4 mm holes (20 × 20 mm pattern) and an M2 screw with a washer goes through each grommet into the frame, so the Lower board floats on rubber 2.5 mm above the plate. The Upper board is not fastened to the frame; it sits on the Lower board’s three standoffs. Here the stack stands on its grommets on the floor grid; “Explode stack” lifts the Upper board and the four screws, leaving the grommets in the Lower board. The orange grommets (generic “M3 to M2” type: 4 mm waist, 6.6 mm flanges), washers and screws are nominal illustrations, not supplier models. No harness wires; the service cable is not drawn on this page.

Standard dark-green display mask, black headers and gold pins are display materials on the KiCad board models; board geometry unchanged. The J1/J2 header toggle preserves the interboard connector.
