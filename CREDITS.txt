StackF765 flight controller renders - credits and licences
==========================================================

Flight controller boards: fc/models/lower.glb, upper.glb, service.glb
  The three boards shown (Lower power board, Upper flight-controller board, Service board) are the
  GLB models exported by KiCad from the StackF765 design files at
  https://github.com/PeterJBurke/stackf765, copied unchanged by sync-models.sh.
  Hardware licence: CERN-OHL-P v2 (see that repository).
  The pages recolour the models at load time (dark-green solder mask, black headers, gold pins,
  plain body colours for parts whose model has no colour). Geometry is not changed.

Page design
  Layout, controls, camera presets, styling and viewer code are taken from Peter Burke's FC viewer at
  https://peterjburke.github.io/dronerenders/fc/ (repository PeterJBurke/dronerenders, commit
  2164bba) and adapted to the StackF765 boards. This site is separate from that repository.

Frame model: fc/assets/native-frame.js
  MOD-L 4-inch HD Cargo frame (4" regular arms, Cargo top and bottom plates, cross plate, 30 mm standoffs).
  Design: MOD-L by ledroneclub, Thingiverse thing 4295478, https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4295478
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY), as stated with the designer's files.
  The packed mesh file is copied unchanged from dronerenders fc/assets/native-frame.js, where the
  designer's part geometry was assembled and recoloured for that viewer (parts not stretched).

Scale coin: fc/assets/penny.js (copied unchanged from dronerenders fc/assets/penny.js)

Matek F405-WMO comparison reference: fc/assets/matek-model.js, matek-drone-assembly.js,
  matek-f405-wmo-original.jpg, matek-model-provenance.json, matek-drone-assembly-provenance.json
  Copied unchanged from dronerenders fc/assets/. A photograph-derived 3D reconstruction, not
  manufacturer CAD; its sources and dimension limits are recorded in the two provenance files.
  The photograph is Matek's product image (https://www.mateksys.com/?portfolio=f405-wmo) and
  remains the property of its owner; it is used here only as the comparison reference.

three.js r160: fc/assets/three.min.js, GLTFLoader.js, BufferGeometryUtils.js
  MIT licence, Copyright 2010-2023 three.js authors; see fc/assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
  three.min.js is copied from dronerenders; the two loader modules are the unmodified r160 example
  modules apart from one relative import path. fc/assets/three-shim.js lets them use the global build.

Mounting posts, rubber grommets, washers, screws and the service cable are simple
illustrative shapes generated in fc/assets/stackf765-loader.js; they are not CAD models.
